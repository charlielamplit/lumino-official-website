# CBAM cadence: quarterly packs vs annual work

- Type: regulations · practice
- Industry: steel / aluminium
- Service: CBAM
- Updated: 2026-10-30

## In one line

Prepare packs to the **buyer’s period**, not to your internal calendar alone. Keep quarterly communication packs separate from annual compliance work.

## Two layers

| Layer | Typical content | Who pushes |
|---|---|---|
| Quarter / shipment | Quantities, codes, interim emission path, communication pack | EU importer, trading desk |
| Annual | Monitoring consistency, facility annual report, verification | Compliance, verifier, HQ |

## A practical plant calendar

1. **Monthly**: book output, fuel, purchased heat/power; flag anomalies
2. **2–3 weeks before quarter end**: reconcile codes and shipments; chase precursors; draft the pack
3. **After buyer confirm**: lock the version; changes get a revision id
4. **Around year-end**: check full-year consistency; prepare verification sampling lists

Legal deadlines follow the importer’s member state and current rules — this is an **internal prep cadence**.
