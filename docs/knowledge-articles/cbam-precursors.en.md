# CBAM precursors: what to split and how to ask suppliers

- Type: how-to
- Industry: steel / aluminium
- Service: CBAM
- Updated: 2026-10-30

## In one line

Much of a finished good’s footprint sits in **bought precursors**. Without them, actuals are wrong and defaults look unexplained.

## Three buckets

| Idea | Meaning | Plant action |
|---|---|---|
| Own-process emissions | On-site combustion / process | Fuel, process gases, meters |
| Bought precursors | Intermediate materials in the good | Trace by purchase lot |
| Complex goods | Several precursors / steps | Split or aggregate under approved rules |

## Five questions for suppliers

1. Does the grade match our purchase lots?
2. What is the quantity / period basis?
3. Actual monitoring or sector default?
4. Does the boundary include purchased heat/power and key auxiliaries?
5. Can they issue a versioned statement or data pack?

## Practical order

1. Rank precursors by emission contribution from BOM / purchases
2. Send one template questionnaire
3. Tag replies: usable / clarify / keep default
4. State the treatment of each precursor in the delivery pack

> Precursor rules follow the current CBAM implementing requirements. Not legal advice.
