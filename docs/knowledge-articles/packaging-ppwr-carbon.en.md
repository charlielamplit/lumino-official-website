# Packaging: handling PPWR and customer carbon questionnaires together

- Type: Q&A
- Industry: packaging
- Service: trade compliance · PCF
- Updated: 2026-10-30

## In one line

PPWR focuses on **design and circularity**; carbon questionnaires focus on **material and footprint data**. Share the materials ledger — don’t merge the conclusions.

## Two ask-sets

| Source | Typical asks | Prepare |
|---|---|---|
| PPWR / packaging rules | Recyclability, recycled content, labelling, roles | Structural BOM, material statements, design changes |
| Customer carbon / PCF | Intensity, boundary, energy and transport assumptions | Activity data, factor versions, boundary note |

## Share one ledger

Specs and grammage, main materials and suppliers, plant energy allocation basis.

## Keep two replies

1. Packaging-compliance reply — cite the policy/clause you actually cover
2. Carbon reply — standard or questionnaire version, boundary, transport in/out

## Four-week checklist

Map destinations and buyer templates → material master → evidence for recycled claims → 1–2 SKU carbon samples, then replicate.

> PPWR detail follows official EU texts and member-state practice. Not legal advice.
