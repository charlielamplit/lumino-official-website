# Organisational GHG inventory: where to start in year one

- Type: methods
- Industry: manufacturing
- Service: inventory
- Updated: 2026-11-02

## In one line

Year one is not perfection. Aim for a **clear boundary**, **solid Scope 1/2**, and **prioritised Scope 3** that you can repeat next year.

## Suggested minimum scope

| Module | Year-one aim | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Organisational boundary | Operational control or equity share on one page | Changing mid-year breaks comparability |
| Scope 1 | Stationary + mobile + known process emissions | Start with major fuels |
| Scope 2 | Purchased electricity (market/location as required) | Align bill periods to the reporting year |
| Scope 3 | Only 2–4 relevant categories | e.g. purchased goods, upstream transport, business travel |

## Six steps

1. Write down boundary and reporting year
2. List sites and energy accounts
3. Collect fuel and electricity evidence; normalise units
4. Choose factor libraries and record versions
5. Pick priority Scope 3 categories and gaps
6. Produce a working inventory pack + short management summary

## Versus PCF / CBAM

Inventory = company year total. PCF = per product. CBAM = listed goods for EU import support. Share ledgers; don’t merge conclusion pages.
