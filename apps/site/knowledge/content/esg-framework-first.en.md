# ESG reporting: framework first, or metrics first?

- Type: methods
- Industry: manufacturing
- Service: ESG
- Updated: 2026-10-30

## In one line

Lock **audience, framework and boundary** before harvesting KPIs. Metric-first almost always reworks.

## Suggested order

1. Audience: regulator, customer questionnaire, lender, or internal
2. Framework: one primary frame — or explicitly “questionnaire response”
3. Organisational boundary
4. Priority topics (5–8), not fifty KPIs at once
5. Data owners and evidence locations
6. Then collect and narrate

## Why teams rework

Wrong format for the audience, mid-year boundary creep, HQ estimates that plants cannot reconcile.

## A two-week minimum

One page: audience + framework + boundary. One table: topics, owners, gaps. Three existing files: energy, H&S, policy list.
