# Organisational GHG: how to split Scope 1, 2 and common Scope 3

- Type: method · Q&A
- Industry: general manufacturing
- Service: GHG inventory
- Updated: 2026-10-30
- Readers: teams doing a first inventory or answering customer carbon questionnaires

## In one line

Separate **what you burn on site, what energy you buy, and what sits up/downstream in the value chain**. A wrong bucket breaks metrics and questionnaires later.

## Three buckets

| Category | Plain meaning | Typical plant examples |
|---|---|---|
| Scope 1 | Direct emissions you own/control | Boiler coal/gas, on-site vehicles, process fugitives |
| Scope 2 | Indirect from purchased electricity, heat, cooling, steam | Grid power, purchased steam |
| Scope 3 | Other indirect value-chain emissions | Purchased materials, upstream transport, commuting, outbound freight, etc. |

> Labels follow the GHG Protocol corporate standard. Whether you calculate a given Scope 3 category depends on your reporting purpose and customer asks.

## How to cut: three questions

1. **Whose organisational boundary?** Own boiler → usually Scope 1; purchased grid power → Scope 2.
2. **Is it a purchased energy carrier?** Bought electricity / steam → Scope 2 rules.
3. **Is it someone else’s activity in your value chain?** Supplier production, carriers → often Scope 3.

## Scope 3 items Chinese plants see most in questionnaires

You do not need all 15 categories at once. Prioritise what the customer names, for example:

- Purchased goods and services (materials)
- Upstream transportation and distribution
- Fuel- and energy-related activities (often alongside 1 and 2)
- Downstream transport (if asked)

Other categories can be “expand next year” — **put that in the plan; do not pretend they are done**.

## Common mix-ups

- Booking purchased electricity as Scope 1
- Mixing supplier emissions into Scope 1 tables without labels
- Treating an organisational inventory and a product footprint as the same exercise

## Suggested order

1. Draw the organisational boundary (which entities / sites)
2. Stabilise Scope 1 + 2 data quality first
3. Pick Scope 3 categories from customer or disclosure needs
4. Document sources and gaps per category

## Anchors

- GHG Protocol Corporate Accounting and Reporting Standard
- ISO 14064-1 (organisational) where used
- Customer questionnaires or exchange / regulatory disclosure rules if applicable
