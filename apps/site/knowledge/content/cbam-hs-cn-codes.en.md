# Is your product covered? HS / CN / TARIC

- Type: how-to
- Industry: steel / aluminium
- Service: CBAM
- Updated: 2026-10-30

## In one line

CBAM coverage is defined by **codes**, not product names. The same “stainless bolt” can land on different codes under different customs practices.

## Three levels

| Level | Digits | Use |
|---|---|---|
| HS | 6 | International classification |
| CN | 8 | EU Combined Nomenclature — CBAM lists use this |
| TARIC | 10 | CN plus import measures |

## Why the same product maps differently

Usually processing depth, material grade, or declared use. Common mistakes:

1. Back-calculating EU codes from export declarations — digits after 8 diverge
2. One SKU spans grades/sizes across a boundary
3. A **precursor** is covered, not the finished good

## How to check

Prefer codes from the EU buyer’s import records. Confirm: on the covered list, which production route, which parameters.

If one shipment spans several codes, **calculate separately** — do not average.

> Use the current implementing act annex for the list. Practical guidance only — not legal advice.
