# Product carbon footprint: collecting activity data and keeping evidence

- Type: methods
- Industry: textiles / packaging / electronics
- Service: PCF
- Updated: 2026-11-02

## In one line

After the boundary is locked, PCF quality hangs on **activity data that matches evidence**. Build a minimum collection list before chasing perfect factors.

## Four data groups

| Group | Typical data | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Materials | Main/aux materials, pack weight, scrap | BOM, receipts, weigh tickets |
| Energy & utilities | Power, steam, fuels, compressed air allocation | Bills, meters, allocation note |
| Transport (if in boundary) | Distance, load, mode | Waybills, logistics summary |
| Waste & recycling | Scrap fate, recycled share | Waste manifests, recycle agreements |

## Two-week order

1. Lock 1–2 SKUs and the functional unit
2. List in-boundary processes (incl. subcontractors)
3. Assign a data owner and evidence location per process
4. Collect what exists; list gaps explicitly
5. Check units, period, and inventory adjustments

## Three quality checks

Can output reconcile to receipts/sales? Is energy allocation metered, by hours, or plant average? Are supplier figures statements, tests, or averages — and which version?

> Follow the agreed ISO 14067 / PCR / buyer spec. Practical guidance only — not assurance or legal advice.
