# After the PCF number: find hotspots and tell a credible reduction story

- Type: Q&A
- Industry: textiles / packaging / electronics / chemicals
- Service: PCF
- Updated: 2026-11-02

## In one line

The single kg CO₂e figure is rarely the end. Buyers want **hotspot structure** and a **verifiable improvement story**.

## Read the structure first

| Pattern | Ask | Next |
|---|---|---|
| Materials-led | Which few materials dominate? | Lightweighting, yield, supplier actuals |
| Energy-led | Process energy or utility allocation? | Efficiency, fuel switch, aligned EACs |
| Transport / waste high | Is the boundary the cause? | Recheck inclusion; optimise logistics/scrap |

## External messaging template

1. Functional unit and boundary in one line
2. Result + up to three hotspots
3. Actions taken or planned (owner + timing)
4. Limits (defaults, gaps, excluded processes)

## Rank reductions by evidence speed

Yield and scrap first → cleaner energy allocation → replace conservative supplier defaults → design changes with BOM updates.

> When comparing years, keep boundary and functional unit constant. Not legal advice.
